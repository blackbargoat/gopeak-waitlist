Before you ask

When does GoPeak launch?+

Very soon. GoPeak is in final review for the App Store. Everyone on the waitlist gets an email the moment it's live.

Is it free?+

Joining the waitlist is free. The app has a free trial so you can try everything before you pay.

Will it actually make me taller?+

No app can make you taller than your genetics. What GoPeak does is show your realistic range and help you build the sleep, nutrition and training habits that help you reach the top of it instead of falling short.

How accurate is the prediction?+

It uses the mid-parental method, the standard starting point doctors use, and shows a range of ±8.5 cm. It's an estimate, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about any growth concerns.

Is it on Android?+

iPhone first. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know if Android comes later.

What do you do with my email?+

We only use it to tell you when GoPeak launches. No spam, no selling it. See our privacy policy.
